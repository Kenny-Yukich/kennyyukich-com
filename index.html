01 Scope of work

Most AI programs fail at the last mile. The model works, the demo lands, and then nobody uses the thing.

I focus on that last mile: turning working models into tools people actually use. Because most of the workforce is on the shop floor rather than at a desk, every tool must prove value in minutes saved, not just technical capability.

I started on the manufacturing floor and taught myself retrieval systems and AI tooling on nights and weekends, then built a personal agent to get up to speed in a new role.
